# BQ Sqwad Share — Brand Hub

**Product name:** BQ Sqwad Share  
**Brand owner and creator:** BQ Sqwad Technologies  
**Company credit:** “Designed and developed by BQ Sqwad Technologies.”

## Brand direction

**Brand line:** Move Anything. Share Anywhere.

A clean, confident, modern identity built around the existing BQ Sqwad blue brand. Keep the supplied master app icon unchanged: do not redraw, recolour, crop, distort, add effects to, or substitute it without approval. Use transparent backgrounds for standalone logo artwork where appropriate.

## Official upload locations

- **Master app icon (the single source of truth):** `branding/source/app-icon/app-icon.png`
- **App logo / horizontal or standalone logo artwork:** `branding/source/app-logo/app-logo.png`
- **Optional monochrome logo:** `branding/source/app-logo/app-logo-mono.png`
- **Current legacy master source:** `branding/logo/logo.png`

The master app icon is the primary brand asset. It should drive launcher icons, adaptive icon artwork, notification artwork, in-app brand marks, and store icon exports. Do not create separate, inconsistent icons in feature screens.

The app logo is a separate supporting asset for places where a larger logo or wordmark fits better. It must remain visually consistent with the master icon.

## Asset standards

- Master app icon: square PNG, preferably 1024×1024 or larger, transparent or solid background as designed.
- App logo: high-resolution PNG with transparent background; keep the original proportions.
- Optional vector originals may be added as SVG/PDF source files.
- Keep original artwork intact. Exported sizes belong in generated output folders, not in place of the source artwork.

## Company attribution

Use **“BQ Sqwad Technologies”** as the company name. Preferred credit: **“Designed and developed by BQ Sqwad Technologies.”** Do not claim a third-party designer or generator as the brand owner.

## Important

The upload folders contain instructions until the final approved artwork is placed there. The Android generator currently reads `branding/logo/logo.png`; after the new master icon is uploaded and approved, update the generator and all app surfaces together so the new source becomes authoritative. Do not delete the legacy source before that migration is verified.

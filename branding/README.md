# BQ Sqwad Share branding

There are exactly two master upload locations:

- `app-identity/app-icon.png`: the single BQ Sqwad Share app icon. This is the source for the launcher, in-app logo, notification mark, and store icon generated by `scripts/generate_branding.py`.
- `company-identity/company-icon.png`: the separate BQ Sqwad Technologies company identity. Use this only where the company itself is named or represented.

Do not upload app artwork into the company folder or company artwork into the app folder. The old `branding/logo/` directory contains legacy/generated assets and is not a new upload location.

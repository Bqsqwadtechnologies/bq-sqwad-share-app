# Data safety (draft answers)

- Data is not sold.
- Files the user sends are not collected by BQ Sqwad Technologies. They move device to device, or to a browser on the same network via a server that runs on the phone.
- Approximate location may be required by Android 12 and below only while discovering nearby devices. It is not stored by the app.
- Photos, videos, audio, files and app list are accessed on device when the user picks them. Not transmitted to us.
- Device or other IDs: the advertising ID is used by AdMob when ads are allowed after consent.
- Crash logs: not collected in this version.
- Data is encrypted in transit for the app session (AES-GCM with a key carried in the QR or code). Play may still require the standard "encrypted in transit" answer for AdMob traffic (HTTPS).
- Users can delete local history in Settings and can uninstall to remove app storage.
- Age: not directed at children. 13+ confirmation in the app.

Content rating: not for under 13. No user-generated public feed. Ads present. No unrestricted web view of the open internet as a product feature. The computer page is a local file list.

# Play listing

Title: BQ Sqwad Share

Short description: Send files to nearby phones. No account and no mobile data.

Full description:
BQ Sqwad Share moves photos, videos, music, documents, apps and other files to phones that are near you. You do not create an account. Transfers stay between the devices you accept.

Find a phone with a live nearby search, a QR code, or a 6-digit code. Pick only the files you want. Watch speed and progress, pause, and try again if a transfer stops. Received files are sorted into folders by type.

Share a folder to a computer on the same Wi-Fi with a PIN, or move a set of files onto a new phone with Phone Clone. History, favorites and a storage cleaner stay on the device.

BQ Sqwad Share is made by BQ Sqwad Technologies.

Keywords: nearby share, file transfer, offline, QR, Wi-Fi, no login, BQ Sqwad

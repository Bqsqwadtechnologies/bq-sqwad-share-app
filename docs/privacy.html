Privacy Policy

This Policy explains how BQ Sqwad Technologies handles information when you use BQ Sqwad Share for Android and its related local sharing features.

Effective date: 7 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 7 November 2026

1. Who we are

BQ Sqwad Share is provided by BQ Sqwad Technologies (“we”, “us” or “our”). For privacy questions or requests, contact us at bqsqwadtechnologies@bqsqwadai.com.

2. How the app works

BQ Sqwad Share is designed for device-to-device file sharing. You choose the files and receiving device. Nearby transfers use the devices’ local wireless connection. Share to a computer provides a PIN-protected page on the phone for a browser on the same local network or hotspot.

We do not operate a BQ Sqwad cloud storage account for transferred files. The current release does not provide a working internet-based long-distance transfer or multi-hop relay network. Supported transfer flows do not upload file contents to a BQ Sqwad file server.

Important: A person who receives a file may keep, copy or share it. Only send files to devices and people you trust.

3. Information handled by the app

Files and transfer details. When you select files, the app accesses the selected content to prepare and complete the transfer you request. It may process file names, sizes, types, progress, integrity information and device connection details. The receiving device gets the content you choose to send.

Information stored on your device. The app stores certain information locally, including transfer history, favorites, app preferences, theme settings, relay preference, and locally approved device names and identifiers. Device Hub creates an installation identifier and exchanges it with a nearby peer during a connection. These records are not a BQ Sqwad online account profile.

Private Vault. If you use Private Vault, files imported into it are encrypted on your device using an Android Keystore-backed key, with access protected by supported biometric security. Vault contents are intended to remain on that device.

Support messages. If you email us, we receive the sender address and the information included in the message so we can respond and handle follow-up.

4. Permissions

  • Nearby Wi-Fi and Bluetooth: to discover and connect to nearby devices. Android may require additional nearby-device or location-related permission depending on its version.
  • Camera: to scan a QR code when you choose that feature; it is not used for continuous recording.
  • Photos, videos, audio and documents: to let you choose content for browsing or sharing. Where available, Android’s system picker limits access to selected items.
  • Folder access: to read the folder you select for Folder Transfer.
  • Notifications: to show transfer or service notifications where enabled and permitted.
  • Network access: to support local network features and advertising or consent services. This does not mean your files are uploaded to our servers.

You can manage permissions in Android Settings. Turning off a permission may prevent its related feature from working.

5. Advertising and consent

The app includes Google AdMob integration. When ads are enabled and available, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, approximate location derived from network information, and ad delivery or measurement data. The exact information depends on your device, region, consent choices and Google’s services.

Where required, the app uses Google’s consent tools to present privacy choices before requesting ads. Available advertising privacy options can be revisited in app Settings. Rewarded advertising is optional. Google handles information collected through its advertising services under its own policies. Read Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. How we use information

We use information handled by the app to provide requested features, connect devices, transfer selected files, maintain local history and preferences, protect connection flows, respond to support requests, meet legal obligations and support advertising where enabled. We do not sell your personal information or use transferred file contents for advertising.

7. When information may be shared

  • Receiving devices: the files and transfer information needed to complete a transfer you initiate.
  • Google: information processed by AdMob and consent services when active.
  • Service providers: information needed to operate a feature or respond to support, subject to appropriate safeguards.
  • Legal and safety matters: where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the public or our legal rights.

8. Retention and deletion

Local history, preferences, favorites and approved-device records remain on your device until you remove them, clear app data or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup settings. Files saved by a receiving device are controlled by that device’s user. Deleting a history entry does not delete a copy already saved elsewhere.

Support emails are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, handle follow-up and meet legal requirements. Advertising providers apply their own retention rules.

9. Security

We use security measures appropriate to the app’s design, including connection controls, transfer integrity checks and Android platform protections. Private Vault uses device-backed encryption. No app, device, wireless connection or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep Android updated, protect your device and verify the recipient before sending sensitive files.

10. Children and age requirements

BQ Sqwad Share is intended for people aged 13 and older. The app asks users to confirm that they meet this minimum age. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

11. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restrictions on certain personal information, and to object to or withdraw consent from certain processing. You can manage Android permissions, clear local app data and use advertising privacy options available in Settings.

To make a privacy request, email bqsqwadtechnologies@bqsqwadai.com. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

12. International use

You may use the app in different countries. Local device-to-device transfers are handled by the connected devices and their network. Google advertising and other providers may process information in countries outside your own, subject to their terms and applicable safeguards.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the app, our practices or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date on this page and take reasonable steps to bring significant changes to users’ attention.

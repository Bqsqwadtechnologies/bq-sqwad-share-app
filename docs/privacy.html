Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

This policy covers the BQ Sqwad Share app for Android, published by BQ Sqwad Technologies. Contact us at bqsqwadtechnologies@bqsqwadai.com.

What the app does with files

Files you send go from your phone to the phone, or the browser on the same Wi-Fi, that you choose. We do not run a server that stores those files, and the app does not upload them to us. A computer link is served by your own phone and stops when you close it.

Transfer history, favorite devices, nicknames, theme choices and the age confirmation stay in local storage on the device. Clearing history in Settings deletes that local list.

Advertising

We show ads with Google AdMob. Google may use the advertising ID to measure and personalize ads where the law and your choices allow it. In the EEA and the UK we use Google’s User Messaging Platform so you can grant or refuse consent before ads are requested. Settings includes Ads privacy options so you can change that choice later. A rewarded ad is optional. If you watch one, the app stores a local timestamp that hides ads, or unlocks Phone Clone and Share to a computer, for 24 hours. We do not sell your files.

Permissions

Nearby Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are used to find phones in the room. On Android 12 and earlier, location access may also be required by the system for that discovery. The camera is used only when you scan a QR code. Notifications are used for finished transfers and, if you leave them on, a single daily tip. We do not ask for broad access to all files. Photos and documents are picked through the system picker.

Other services

The app can open the Play Store to rate BQ Sqwad Share or to show another of our apps. In-app updates use Google Play’s update API. Crash reports are not collected unless we later add a crash tool and you agree. If we add one, we will name it here before it runs.

Children

BQ Sqwad Share is not for children under 13. The first screen asks you to confirm that you are 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Retention and your choices

Because files and history stay on the device, you can delete them yourself, or clear the app’s storage in Android settings. That removes local history, settings and received copies the app saved in its own folders. Copies you already saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

If you write to us, we keep the email long enough to answer and to handle a follow-up, then delete it when we no longer need it for that conversation.

International use

You can install the app outside the country where we operate. Ad requests are handled by Google under its own terms. Device-to-device transfers do not pass through our systems.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and ship the new text with the app’s Settings link. Continued use after that update means you have the new policy.
